Abstract
--------
This study investigates the transcriptional response of demo plants under salt stress.
RNA-seq analysis identified DEMO_GENE_A, DEMO_GENE_B, and DEMO_GENE_C as key stress-responsive genes.
Bioassay results confirmed dose-dependent effects of demo compounds.

Introduction
------------
Demo plants are an established model for abiotic stress research.
The molecular mechanisms remain incompletely understood.
This study uses transcriptomic profiling and bioassay approaches.

Methods
-------
RNA-seq was performed on demo leaf tissue under control and salt stress conditions.
Differential expression analysis identified significantly regulated genes (Table S1).
Bioassay experiments measured the lethal concentration (LC50) of demo compound X (Table S2).
Supplementary Methods provide detailed protocols.

Results
-------
Figure S1 shows the experimental workflow.
Differential expression analysis revealed 500 significantly regulated genes (Table S1),
including DEMO_GENE_A (log2FC = 3.2, padj = 0.001), DEMO_GENE_B (log2FC = -2.1, padj = 0.008),
and DEMO_GENE_C (log2FC = 1.5, padj = 0.03).

Bioassay results (Table S2) show that demo compound X has an LC50 of 12.5 μg/mL
against demo test organisms with 95% CI of [10.2, 14.8].

Discussion
----------
DEMO_GENE_A shows the strongest upregulation under salt stress,
suggesting a role in stress adaptation. The bioassay data demonstrate
concentration-dependent effects of demo compound X.

The expression data in Table S1 provide a comprehensive view of the transcriptomic
response. The LC50 results in Table S2 confirm the bioactivity of demo compound X.
